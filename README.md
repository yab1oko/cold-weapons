# Cold Weapons

Cold Weapons is a melee weapon pack built for zombie apocalypse maps, survival packs, and any world where fancy enchanted swords feel out of place.

Scavenge what the old world left behind. Crowbars, bats, kitchen knives, rusty pipes, stop signs, wrenches — everyday junk turned into something that can keep you alive. The roster mixes light and fast weapons with slow, heavy hitters, plus a few tools that still dig, mine, and chop.

**Features**

- 20+ improvised melee weapons with custom 3D models

**Weapon types**

- Blunt force: hammers, bats, pan, rolling pin, knuckles, stop sign
- Sharp / stabbing: knives, cleaver, screwdriver, broken bottle, crowbar
- Reach / heavy: rake, sledgehammer, axe, wrench
- Scrap tools: rusty pipe, hand saw, pickaxe, shovel

**Notes**

Cold Weapons focuses on melee gear and feel. It does not add a full progression system or gun content — just the weapons you would actually find (or craft from scrap) after the world ends.

Feedback and balance suggestions are welcome.
