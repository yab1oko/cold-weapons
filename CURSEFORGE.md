# Cold Weapons

Cold Weapons is a melee weapon pack built for zombie apocalypse maps, survival packs, and any world where fancy enchanted swords feel out of place.

Scavenge what the old world left behind. Crowbars, bats, kitchen knives, rusty pipes, stop signs, wrenches — everyday junk turned into something that can keep you alive. The roster mixes light and fast weapons with slow, heavy hitters, plus a few tools that still dig, mine, and chop.

Most weapons are **found**: placed in the world, or handed out by map loot. A few special ones are **crafted** when [Roleplay Materials](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/roleplay-materials) is installed (circular saw, chainsaw sword, taser battery and shield planks, machete, forged spear, forged mace, nailed bat, knife spear). The bat, stone axe, and stone spear also have their own crafting recipes. Zombie drops exist in the config and are **optional and off by default**.

**Features**

- 20+ improvised melee weapons with custom 3D models

- Balanced damage, attack speed, and durability per weapon
- Throwable knives: kitchen knife, cleaver, and military knife. **Sneak, hold right-click, then release.** A longer hold throws harder. Headshots deal bonus damage. Other items can be added in `throwableWeapons`; knives from this mod do not need to be listed.

- **Circular saw:** hold right-click to spin. You hold it in front with both hands, and the blade faces forward. Spinning spends a charge pool (150 by default, 1.5 per second). A Roleplay Materials battery restores 100.
- **Chainsaw sword:** right-click toggles the blade. Idle damage is 4, running damage is 8. Running spends the same charge pool. Recharge with a battery in the inventory or the off-hand.
- **Taser:** right-click fires one dart. Two wires run back to the taser. A hit stuns (5 seconds by default). Walk out of range and the stun ends. A yellow bar is the reload (18 seconds). A battery restores durability.
- **Wooden shield:** blocks like a vanilla shield. Repair it in an anvil with a Roleplay Materials plank.
- Optional [Better Combat](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/better-combat): attack animations and weapon movesets when that mod is installed
- Optional JEI: an info page on every weapon

**Weapon types**

- Blunt: hammer, sledgehammer, bat, nailed bat, pan, rolling pin, knuckles, stop sign, forged mace
- Sharp: kitchen knife, cleaver, military knife, screwdriver, broken bottle, crowbar, machete, hand saw
- Reach and tools: rake, wrench, rusty pipe, stone axe, stone spear, knife spear, forged spear, pickaxe, shovel, axe
- Special: circular saw, chainsaw sword, taser, wooden shield

**Notes**

Cold Weapons focuses on melee gear and feel. It does not add a full progression system or gun content — just the weapons you would actually find (or craft from scrap) after the world ends.

Feedback and balance suggestions are welcome.
